# FlowDesk

FlowDesk is a desktop workspace application for managing daily work, projects,
tasks, team activity, schedules, messages, and progress in one place.

It gives users a clear view of what is happening across the workspace so they
can stay organized, track work completion, and collaborate with their team more
easily.

## What You Can Do With FlowDesk

- **View your workspace dashboard** - See projects, active tasks, team members,
  revenue, recent activity, task progress, and team performance at a glance.
- **Manage projects** - Open project lists, review project details, and keep
  project work organized.
- **Track tasks** - View active tasks, check task details, and follow completion
  progress.
- **Stay connected with your team** - Use member profiles, chat, and email
  sections to keep communication close to the work.
- **Plan your schedule** - Use the calendar to review upcoming events and stay
  aware of important dates.
- **Monitor team activity** - Review recent workspace activity and top
  performers from the dashboard.
- **Manage your account** - Access your profile, change your password, and
  update settings when needed.
- **Recover access** - Use the forgot password option if you cannot sign in.
- **Receive app updates** - FlowDesk can notify you when a new version is
  available, helping you stay current with the latest improvements.

## Main Sections

### Dashboard

The dashboard is the first place to understand the current state of your
workspace. It shows important numbers, activity charts, task breakdowns, recent
events, and team performance.

### Projects

The Projects section helps you view ongoing work and open individual project
details when you need more information.

### Tasks

The Tasks section helps you follow work items, review task details, and keep
track of progress.

### Calendar

The Calendar section helps you keep an eye on upcoming events and scheduled
work.

### Email

The Email section keeps workspace messages available inside FlowDesk, including
unread message tracking.

### Chat

The Chat section helps you continue team conversations without leaving the app.

### Members

The Members section gives you access to team member information and member
detail pages.

### Profile And Settings

The Profile, Change Password, and Settings sections help you manage your own
account and application preferences.

## Getting Started

1. Open FlowDesk on your desktop.
2. Sign in with your account.
3. Start from the dashboard to review your workspace summary.
4. Use the sidebar or navigation menu to move between Projects, Tasks, Calendar,
   Email, Chat, Members, Profile, and Settings.

## Account Help

If you forget your password, use the **Forgot Password** option on the sign-in
screen. FlowDesk will guide you through the recovery process.

## Updates

When a new version of FlowDesk is available, the app can show an update notice.
Install updates when prompted to get the latest fixes and improvements.
